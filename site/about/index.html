About us

Established in NSW, Australia in 2017, Chiron Global Tech has developed world-leading training and operational body armour equipment for Defence, Law Enforcement, Federal Agencies, Security and Corrections personnel.

Utilising advanced manufacturing and advanced materials technologies, we work closely with end-users and subject matter experts to develop innovative, leading-edge solutions for frontline personnel solving common issues with training and operational equipment.

Extensive research, development and testing by Chiron’s engineering, design and combatives teams has resulted in a new approach to personal protective equipment, with an emphasis on training with high levels of reality for a variety of scenarios.

Our flagship product the Chiron-X1™ Elite, a high-impact, close quarter battle (CQB) training suit, is currently in use with Tier 1 Special Forces and other military units around the world.

Widely acknowledged by subject matter experts as the world’s only training suit enabling highly realistic CQB training and extreme close quarters shooting of Non-Lethal Training Munitions (NLTM), the Chiron-X1 Elite significantly improves survivability, reduces injuries and better prepares frontline personnel for high-risk operational scenarios.

Combatives training

Train as they Fight

Our patented, protective training solution is the world’s first hard-plate combatives training suit. The unique design enables personnel to truly ‘Train as they Fight’ using realistic force-on-force strikes to vital targets with high levels of safety. The Chiron-X1 Series reduces training injuries, eliminates training scars from incorrect muscle memory, and fully prepares personnel for violent operational encounters.

The Chiron-X1™ is lightweight and agile, with unparalled levels of protection and coverage for the role player. It is ideal for high fidelity training, enabling seamless ‘force continuum escalation’ and simultaneous hot and cold weapons drills including full force strikes to vital targets. This enhanced training realism reinforces correct muscle memory and saves lives in the field.

PAUL CALE – CEO, KINETIC FIGHTING AND CREATOR OF THE ADF’S ARMY COMBATIVES PROGRAM

Capabilities

Training, certification and performance.

01

CLOSE QUARTER BATTLE TRAINING

We engage subject matter expert veterans to train and demonstrate the X1 capability to end users. Our team of trainers come from special forces/military and law enforcement backgrounds including LE tactical operations roles. All Chiron trainers are certified for ‘Train the trainer’ training in the safe use and most effective training techniques using the Chiron-X1 suits. They are all highly experienced in providing practical training and techniques that better protect and prepare personnel for frontline roles.

02

TRAIN THE TRAINER (TTT) CERTIFICATION

The Chiron-X1 training system enables trainers to escalate training to unprecedented levels, practising lethal techniques while keeping the role player well protected. These training techniques have been developed in conjunction with some of the world’s most experienced CQB experts and incorporated into our TTT certification - a one-day course that is offered with purchase or leasing of our Chiron X-Series suits.

We have feedback and techniques gained from training with dozens of special forces units around the world which we have incorporated into an insights paper with key learnings and improvements for CQB training. These insights are available to customers as part of our TTT certification.

03

HUMAN PERFORMANCE OPTIMISATION

The Chiron-X1 is designed to be used with biometric monitoring systems to capture key human performance indicators. We partner with Rendersafe, a veteran run company that delivers cutting edge solutions for EOD and SOF Operators through their unique three-pillar approach: Training, Special Effects (SFX) and Human Performance Optimisation (HPO).

Rendersafe offer a range of services that encompass the latest advancements in physiological monitoring, biotech, pharmaco-nutrition, and performance testing. Combining live immersion and simulation exercises that include the Chiron-X1 suits for high-fidelity training offers a highly detailed insight into personnel’s ability to perform under pressure and recover, combined with progress markers to measure improvement.

04

PACKAGES

We offer the Chiron-X1 training capability via rental, leasing or purchase. Many of our customers choose to undertake a short rental to test the capability before committing to a longer term lease or purchase. We are able to tailor packages including TTT options to meet your specific requirements.

Technical

Design and manufacture

DESIGN AND MANUFACTURE

Chiron is proudly Australian-owned. All engineering design and prototyping is carried out at our in-house Research and Development facility by a team of composites engineers, armour design and fabrication specialists. Large scale manufacture is undertaken in Five Eyes jurisdictions including the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Over five years of extensive research, development and testing have gone into Chiron’s patented, high-tech armour system, culminating in leading-edge products constructed of a combination of advanced composite, high-performance materials, designed to maximise protection while ensuring very high levels of agility and mobility.

TESTING AND CERTIFICATION

Chiron’s product prototypes have undergone significant testing, including CQC impact testing, agility and speed tests and field testing with Naval Boarding and Clearance units, Military Police, Law Enforcement Tactical Ops teams, Special Forces and frontline infantry personnel. A range of blunt force impact tests, steel ball drop tests, and destruction testing has been undertaken to develop and optimise the design of the suit and the performance of the materials.

All Chiron products undergo rigorous testing and safety trials, prior to being distributed and are independently tested to ensure they meet relevant safety standards.
