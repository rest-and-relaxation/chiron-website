Training partners

Kinetic Fighting

Kinetic Fighting specialises in preparing front-line personnel for success in high-end, extreme-risk operations and has set the standard for many years in close-quarter tactical training for armed forces, law enforcement and other organisations operating on the front line. Kinetic's team of close-quarter combat (CQC) subject-matter experts, led by former Australian Defence Force Special Forces operator, Paul Cale, provides hands-on instruction on how to most effectively utilise the Chiron-X1™ training armour for CQC and Non-Lethal Training Munitions (NLTM) training.

European Security Academy

Founded in 1992 in Poland by Dr. Andrzej Bryl, ESA began as a specialist training facility for special forces using Dr Bryl's unique BAS-3 combat system. In 2008 ESA expanded to meet the growing demand for state-of-the-art expert protection and shooting training, offering specialist courses for military, law enforcement and private security individuals from around the world. With over 25 years of experience, the ESA is a global authority in close security and protection training, providing year-round courses in its 150-hectare facility in Poland.

Visit website Poland — 150 ha facility

T1 Combatives

Founded in 2025, T1 Combatives is dedicated to delivering specialised, reality-based training solutions for front-line professionals operating in high-risk environments. With a background in developing and refining Close Quarter Tactics for Singapore's SAF Special Forces, T1 Combatives combines combat-proven methodologies with a mission-focused mindset.

Rendersafe

Rendersafe is a veteran run company incorporating cutting-edge capabilities such as advanced medical training, SFX and HPO to monitor the performance, health, and resilience of EOD and SOF Operators in demanding and high-stress environments.

Rendersafe offers a comprehensive range of services that encompass the latest advancements in physiological monitoring, biotech, pharmaco-nutrition, and performance testing. Combined with live immersion and simulation exercises, this offers a highly detailed insight into personnel's ability to perform under pressure and recover, combined with progress markers to measure improvement.
