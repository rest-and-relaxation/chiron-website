X1 ELITE

High impact CQB training armour

Designed for frontline military, special forces and police tactical operations units the Chiron-X1™ Elite Close Quarter Battle (CQB) training suit enables training for lethality, preparing personnel to face highly violent operational scenarios.

The Chiron-X1™ Elite is manufactured from a proprietary blend of carbon fibre composite materials. Our unique design combines unparalleled protection levels with superior levels of agility.

Ideal for high-fidelity training or scenarios requiring simulated lethality such as CQC, CQB, room entry, breaching, and boarding and clearance drills.

The X1-Elite takes CQB training to a completely new level of realism, allowing personnel to really ‘Train as they Fight’ using realistically high levels of force and speed using real weapons or training weapons, and point-blank shooting with non-lethal training munitions.

X-Series / X1H — Hybrid

CHIRON-X1H (HYBRID)

Training armour

The Chiron-X1H™ (Hybrid) Close Quarter Battle (CQB) suit offers many of the same features as the Chiron-X1 Elite at a more affordable pricepoint. With similar performance to the Chiron-X1 Elite, the Chiron-X1H is designed to fit into current military, law enforcement and corrections budgets.

The Chiron-X1™ provides high levels of protection, with carbon fibre over the vital targets and injection moulded proprietary materials over non-vital targets. The Chiron-X1H is ideal for training for rapid decision making and full force continuum escalation, including the use of Non-lethal Training Munitions (NLTM) at point blank range.

X-Series / X1R — Riot suit

CHIRON-X1R

Public order duty armour

The Chiron-X1R™ Riot suit is a highly protective, active duty, full body armour system designed especially for Law Enforcement / Public Order and Corrections personnel. The Chiron-X1R™ Riot suit is ideal for civil unrest, riot protection and highly dangerous scenarios including violent prisoner cell extraction / transportation, and high-risk security applications.

Lightweight and extremely manoeuvrable, the Chiron-X1R™ offers extensive coverage against a range of threats including heavy blunt weapons, bladed weapons, spikes and projectiles. Designed to withstand high levels of force and speed of attacks to vital targets such as the head, neck and chest, the X1R offers enhanced levels of coverage and protection over traditional riot protection equipment.

The unique design of the Chiron-X1R is optimised to ensure equal high protection levels and coverage for both male and female personnel. Available in a range of sizes.

We are currently taking pre-orders for the Chiron-X1R, with availability in 2027 — please contact us for information.
